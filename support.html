Solving a puzzle

Type the word you guessed, then tap each tile until it matches the colour your puzzle showed: red for a letter that is not in the word, amber for a letter in the word but in the wrong place, green for a letter in the right place. Submit, and the app shows how many words still fit and which guesses narrow things down fastest.

  • Five or six letters: switch at the top of the screen. Each length keeps its own puzzle.
  • Why these suggestions: tap the info button beside the suggestions to see why each one ranks where it does.
  • What a word means: touch and hold a guess or a suggestion, then choose Define.
  • Work it out yourself: tap the eye to hide the suggestions and keep just the word count and the keyboard.
  • Tagged a tile wrongly? Undo brings your last guess back to fix.
  • Review your guesses: once a puzzle is solved, tap Review Your Guesses to see each guess beside the one the solver would have played, and why. It's the same review practice gives, for a puzzle you played somewhere else.

Solving is free, with no limit on puzzles. Reviewing a solved puzzle is part of Practice Coach, and your first three reviews are free.

Practice Coach

Tap the mortarboard beside Settings to practise. Guess Grid picks a word and you have six guesses to find it. When the game ends, every guess is set beside the one the solver would have played, with the habit a guess showed and when luck decided a turn. Before your first guess you can turn on Hard mode (letters you find must be used) or Trap words (words from families like LIGHT, MIGHT and NIGHT). After 10 games at a length played without suggestions, the clipboard shows coaching from your own games, along with the games themselves. Against the solver, under your statistics, shows how many guesses each recent game took compared with the solver on the same word.

Your first three practice games are free, and so are your first three reviews of puzzles solved in the solver; the two are counted separately. After that, Practice Coach is a one-time in-app purchase for unlimited practice, reviews and coaching. There is no subscription.

  • Restoring the purchase: Settings › Practice Coach › Restore Purchases. It unlocks on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

It says no words match my clues.
Usually a tile is tagged with the wrong colour. Tap Undo to bring the last guess back and correct it. It can also mean your puzzle used a word that is not in the app's list.
Why does it suggest a word that cannot be the answer?
Words marked "Narrows it down" cannot win this turn, but testing them rules out more possibilities than guessing an answer outright. The info button beside the suggestions shows why.
I can't see the info button.
It appears while the suggestions are showing and more than two words are left. If you've hidden the suggestions, tap the eye to show them.
What do the percentages on the keyboard mean?
How many of the remaining words contain that letter. The blue shows something different: how much a guess using that letter would narrow things down. It's strongest when a letter is in about half the remaining words, because then either answer rules out a lot. A letter in 80% of words is likely, but testing it usually just confirms it, so its key is a paler blue than one at 40%.
Coaching only shows a progress bar.
Coaching starts after 10 practice games at a length played without suggestions; five and six letters are counted separately. A game where the suggestions were showing at any point isn't counted, and is marked Suggestions in your games list, so coaching reflects what you worked out yourself.
Review Your Guesses opens the Practice Coach screen.
Your three free reviews have been used. Reopening the review of a puzzle you've already reviewed is always free, including switching it to hard mode. Practice Coach unlocks unlimited reviews.
What is "Played in hard mode" in a review?
The solver can't tell whether you played your puzzle in hard mode. If you did, switch it on: each guess is then compared only with the words the rule allowed, which can change its grade. It appears only when your guesses followed the rule.
The review says a guess had tiles tagged differently.
The review works out every guess's colours from the answer, your last guess, and names any guess you tagged differently. Its grades use the answer's colours, so they're still right.
The review says the answer isn't in Guess Grid's word list.
Your puzzle used a word the app doesn't have, so the solver couldn't suggest it. The review still grades your guesses, counting the answer among the words that fit, but leaves out the solver's own guess count.
What does Against the solver show?
Your recent practice games at that length, each sorted by how many more guesses it took than the solver on the same word: fewer, the same, one, two, or three or more, and those not solved. Games played with suggestions are left out, as in coaching.
A guess was rejected in hard mode.
In hard mode, every letter you've found must be used. The line under the grid says what's missing, and the rejected guess doesn't use up a turn.
My purchase didn't unlock Practice Coach.
Try Settings › Practice Coach › Restore Purchases, signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. If Ask to Buy is on, it unlocks once the purchase is approved.
How do I clear my games or statistics?
On the Coaching screen, Clear removes the games at that length. In practice, Reset beside your statistics clears the totals. Neither affects the other, or your purchase.
How do I look up a word?
Touch and hold any guess or suggestion and choose Define. Definitions come from dictionaries iOS has downloaded: if none has the word, choose Manage Dictionaries on the screen that opens to add one.
