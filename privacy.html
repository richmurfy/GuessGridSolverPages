Guess Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

The short version

Guess Grid collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Nothing you type leaves your device.

What the app stores

One setting is saved on your device: whether suggested words are shown or hidden. It stays on the device, is not readable by anyone else, and is deleted when you delete the app. Guesses you enter are held in memory only and are gone when you close the app or start a new puzzle.

Word definitions

If you tap a word to see its definition, the app asks iOS to show its own built-in dictionary. The lookup is handled by the operating system, and the app is not told the result.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released.
