Guess Grid — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version

Guess Grid collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it keeps stays on your iPhone. Its only network connection is to Apple's App Store, for Practice Coach: to show its price, and to check, buy or restore the purchase.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Settings: the puzzle length you last used, whether suggestions are shown, and your practice choices (hints, the heatmap keyboard, hard mode and trap words).
  • Practice: the word you're working on, your statistics for each length, and your last 100 practice games, with each guess and its review. Coaching is worked out from these games on the device.
  • Practice Coach: how many free games you've played and whether you've bought it.

Guesses you enter in the solver are held in memory only and are gone when you close the app or start a new puzzle.

None of this is sent anywhere, and other apps can't read it. You can clear it yourself: Clear on the Coaching screen removes the games at that length, and Reset beside your practice statistics clears the totals. Deleting the app deletes everything. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's own backups (to iCloud or a computer), which belong to you; the developer can't see them.

Buying Practice Coach

Practice Coach is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under Apple's privacy policy. The app asks the App Store, through Apple's StoreKit, for Practice Coach's price and whether your Apple Account owns it, and checks the answer on the device. No payment details, name or email address reach the app or the developer.

Sharing a game

The Share button in practice creates a short text summary of your game. It goes only where you choose to send it, through the iOS share sheet.

Word definitions

If you tap a word to see its definition, the app asks iOS to show its own built-in dictionary. The lookup is handled by the operating system, and the app is not told the result.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to the developer. That is controlled by iOS, not the app, and can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released. This update describes practice, coaching and Practice Coach, which keep their data on the device only.
